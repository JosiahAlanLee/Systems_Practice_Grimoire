Clavicula Matricis

Verso · Tabula XIII

Sketching. Any sketch made with the practice follows one rule: a sketch prompts a claim and never replaces one. Revision 3’s twelve sketching marks remain available, provided each mark’s meaning is stated every time it is used.

The mark. A loose curl with no central dot, turning outward with space between turns, its outer end left open, and a short branch curving away into its own open end. It suggests returning to a question from a changed position, with room to diverge or stop. Rotation and proportion carry no doctrine; Le Guin’s Kesh suggested the orientation, and this is not a Kesh glyph.

Orientation.

· Keep models contestable.

· Make purposes and exclusions visible.

· Protect reporting and the capacity to act on it.

· Know who controls what fades, and who else can read the clock.

· Count the costs of action and of inaction.

· Keep care independent of earned worth.

· Let the practice itself be revised or abandoned. No one needs permission to stop using it, and no service may depend on adherence.

This sentence was omitted from the small copies for want of room.
