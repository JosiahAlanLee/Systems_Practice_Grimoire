Verso · Tabula II

Start the first when the break is recognized. Stop it when the work is sent. Let the second continue until the work is done. Enter the names of these moments before entering their distance.

Do not weigh the stone by the time spent asking to lift it. Do not suppose that every hand beyond the gate has permission to proceed.

If the account provides only one hour, preserve one hour. A second dial drawn upon the leaf will not recover the missing witness.
