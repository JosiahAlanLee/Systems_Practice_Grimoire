Clavicula Matricis

Verso · Tabula XI

I followed each line with the point lifted clear of the paper. Both arrived at the same closed place. I cut a passage beside it and repeated the course. This time the point reached the lower gate.

I had repaired the figure exactly as intended. The intended repair was insufficient.

The lower gate is present in every copy. You have no warrant to remove it.

That tells me how long it has been there.

The new keeper read the correction as an order.

xi v

Leave the neck open.
