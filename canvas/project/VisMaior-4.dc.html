The Operation

Take the clause as it is written:

NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE

Strike every letter that has come before:

NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE

Twelve remain. Lay them one upon another, and keep what the figure needs:

N E I T H R P A Y S L B

It needs no charging. The clause was signed, filed and forgotten, and that was the charge.
