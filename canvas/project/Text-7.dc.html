§ 7

Ten constraints

These bound every application of the practice, including applications to itself.

1. No subsystem speaks as Reason as a whole.

2. Standing follows interests, not usefulness.

3. Consent alone does not establish freedom from domination.

4. Claims of domination remain answerable to counterevidence, including the testimony of those said to be dominated.

5. Emancipation permits no permanent or hereditary outgroup.

6. Material dependence is investigated for uncontrolled power.

7. Collective obligation grants no unrestricted jurisdiction.

8. Uncertain standing does not load all error onto those possibly excluded.

9. Irreversible destruction carries a proportionate burden of evidence.

10. Critical decisions require effective, affordable contestation that survives the refusal or capture of the relevant gatekeeper.

Constraint 10 asks whether contestation actually works against the specific capture in question, not whether every dependency is duplicated. Declare shared risks and test actual correction.
