Clavicula Matricis

Verso · Tabula IV

The first copy had a pale place beneath the seal. I left a pale place beneath mine. When the third keeper asked why, I told her that the master had reserved it.

Thereafter no one wrote there. The reserved place passed intact through six hands. Everything around it was amended.

The copies agree on the size of the blank. That is the extent of their agreement.

The first sheet had been patched. I cannot tell you what the patch covered.
